# Learn Digital Marketing with ChatGPT 🚀

A free, beginner-friendly guide to learning digital marketing basics using ChatGPT as your tutor. No experience needed.

> ChatGPT is great for learning and practice, but it is not a data source. Always check real numbers in tools like Google Search Console and Google Keyword Planner.

## Who is this for?

- Complete beginners in digital marketing
- Students looking for a simple study plan
- Freelancers and small business owners who want to learn the basics

## How to use this repo

1. Follow the 30-day checklist below.
2. Copy a prompt, paste it into ChatGPT, and replace the [brackets].
3. Do the task yourself first, then ask ChatGPT for feedback.
4. Tick off each topic when you finish.

## 30-Day Learning Checklist

- [ ] **Week 1:** SEO basics (keywords, on-page SEO, search intent)
- [ ] **Week 2:** Content and social media marketing
- [ ] **Week 3:** Email marketing and paid ads basics
- [ ] **Week 4:** Analytics, then build one small real project

## Starter Prompts

### Get a roadmap
```text
I'm a complete beginner. Create a 30-day plan to learn digital marketing, with 1 hour of study per day.
```

### Understand any topic
```text
Explain [SEO / email marketing / Google Ads] in simple words, like I'm a beginner. Add one real-life example.
```

### Practice keyword research
```text
Give me 20 long-tail keyword ideas for a [your topic] blog and group them by search intent.
```

### Plan social media content
```text
Create a 7-day Instagram content plan for a [business type]. Explain why each post works.
```

### Get feedback on your work
```text
Here's my blog intro: [paste text]. What's unclear, and how can I make it more interesting?
```

### Test yourself
```text
Ask me 10 questions about [topic], one at a time, and tell me if my answer is right or wrong.
```

## What ChatGPT Can and Can't Do

| ✅ Good for | ❌ Not good for |
|---|---|
| Explaining concepts simply | Real search volume or CPC data |
| Brainstorming ideas | Current Google rankings |
| Quizzing you | Latest algorithm updates |
| Reviewing your drafts | Replacing real practice |
